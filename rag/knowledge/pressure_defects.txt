PRESSURE-RELATED DEFECTS AND CORRECTIVE ACTIONS
=================================================

1. HIGH PRESSURE DEFECTS (Pressure > 5.5 bar)
----------------------------------------------
Causes:
- Blockage in outlet or flow channel (flash, solidified material).
- Pump over-pressurizing due to worn pressure regulator.
- Viscosity increase (cold material, wrong grade).
- Clogged filter or screen pack.

Resulting defects:
- Flashing: excess material extruded at mold parting line.
- Cracking or stress fractures in finished part.
- Tooling and die damage due to over-clamping.
- Dimensional inaccuracy (oversized parts).
- Accelerated mold wear.

Corrective actions for HIGH pressure:
1. Reduce injection/feed pressure setpoint by 0.5–1.0 bar increments.
2. Check for and clear any blockage in the flow channel.
3. Inspect and replace clogged screen pack or filter.
4. Verify material viscosity; if too high, slightly increase barrel temperature.
5. Check pressure transducer calibration.
6. Inspect mold clamping force; ensure mold is properly closed and locked.
7. Replace worn pressure regulator or relief valve.

2. LOW PRESSURE DEFECTS (Pressure < 4.5 bar)
---------------------------------------------
Causes:
- Pump wear or failure.
- Hydraulic fluid leak.
- Pressure regulator set too low.
- Worn cylinder seals.

Resulting defects:
- Short shots: incomplete part fill.
- Sink marks due to insufficient pack pressure.
- Voids and porosity inside the part.
- Weak weld lines.
- Poor surface finish.

Corrective actions for LOW pressure:
1. Increase pressure setpoint by 0.3–0.5 bar and monitor effect.
2. Inspect hydraulic lines for leaks; repair or replace damaged lines.
3. Check pump output pressure; service or replace worn pump.
4. Inspect and replace worn cylinder seals.
5. Verify pressure sensor accuracy with a reference gauge.

3. PRESSURE MONITORING BEST PRACTICES
---------------------------------------
- Monitor both injection and cavity pressure if possible.
- Calibrate pressure transducers every 6 months.
- Keep a pressure trend log; sudden spikes indicate blockage.
- Pressure drops of >0.5 bar from baseline warrant immediate investigation.
- Use a pressure relief valve set to 110% of maximum operating pressure.
